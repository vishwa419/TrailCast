About Trail Weather

Trail Weather helps hikers plan their adventures by providing accurate weather forecasts for popular hiking destinations. Our service combines weather data with trail information to help you choose the best days for your outdoor activities.

We use open data from the Open-Meteo API to provide reliable weather forecasts for locations around the world. All images used on this site are freely available under open licenses from Wikimedia Commons and other sources.

Trail Weather is an open-source project. We don't use any proprietary APIs or services that require API keys.
